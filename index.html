The graph uses a simplified mathematical model of gradual alcohol absorption and elimination. It illustrates a possible rise, peak and decline rather than measuring your actual BAC.
Safety warning: ClearTime provides mathematical estimates only. Alcohol absorption and elimination vary substantially between people and circumstances. Food, drinking speed, medications, health and many other factors can change actual BAC. Do not use this app to decide whether you are safe or legally able to drive, cycle, operate machinery or perform any safety-critical activity. If there is any doubt, do not drive.
