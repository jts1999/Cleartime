Safety warning: ClearTime provides mathematical estimates only. Actual BAC and alcohol elimination vary significantly between people. Do not use this estimate to decide whether you are safe or legally able to drive, cycle, operate machinery, work at height or perform any safety-critical activity. If there is any doubt, do not drive.
